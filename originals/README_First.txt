HOW IT WORKS
  1. Mount the new V2 cover on the Vader 5 Pro.
  2. Slide Box 2's underside dovetail rail into the female receiver.
  3. Insert the purple locking key at the positive-Y end of the receiver.
  4. To reverse Box 2's USB-C direction, remove the key, slide the box out,
     rotate the box 180 degrees in the mounting plane, slide it in again,
     and reinsert the key. The same Box 2 fits your G7 Pro V22 mount.

DESIGN / VALIDATION
  Units: mm. Source Flydigi mounting cover from user-supplied 85盖.3mf
  (print-selected, watertight cover geometry).
  Universal receiver, Box 2 and key re-used from latest G7 Pro V22 STLs.
  The added exterior bridge occupies Z >= 5.0 and does not alter the
  controller-facing latch geometry on the underside of the V2 cover.
  Mount STL checked as single, watertight, connected component.
  Both normal and 180-degree rotated Box 2 assembled poses checked with
  3D solid intersection against the mounted receiver (no intersections).
  G7 V22 locking key has minor intentional contact with the box rail.
  3MF re-opened with three separate watertight printable objects.

IMPORTANT
  This is a designed prototype, not a physically test-fitted mount.
  Test the plastic receiver and latch before trusting it during gameplay;
  do not force an interference fit. PETG is recommended for snap flexibility.
  The opening in Box 2 points outward in both mounting orientations.
