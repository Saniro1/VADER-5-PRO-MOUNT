# Physical fit feedback after V28 PLA

Owner reports both V28 A and B fail to sit flush or click at the bottom; B is slightly better. Owner explicitly confirms all support material was removed. The support-remnant hypothesis must not be treated as the explanation for this test.

Audit of `tools/build_v28_geometry.py`: underside relief restores five hook/root regions from V26 A. Its 0.15/0.30 mm relief is not applied uniformly to those protected regions. Sampled shell-thickness checks do not measure OEM clearance or prove fit. The original supplied cover is not verified OEM CAD. The OEM central T-shaped feature's mechanical purpose is unknown from photos.

Comparison photos establish qualitative profile differences but lack a dimensional reference and an orthogonal view. No further geometry revision is released from these photos alone. Need a scaled underside and side-profile comparison or caliper measurements of OEM shell/rim thickness and hook clearances to identify the next local correction.

[Printable reference sheet](OEM_Profile_Reference.pdf): print A4 at 100%, verify its 50 mm bar, and follow camera directions. A millimeter ruler immediately beside the parts is an alternative. Photos remain approximate due to depth and perspective. User photographs are not published in this repository.
